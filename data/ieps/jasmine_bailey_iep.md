## Present Levels — Academics

### English Language Arts

**Current Performance:**
Jasmine is at a third-grade reading level, as measured by iReady in Fall 2025. Her subcategory scores are Vocabulary: Grade 3, Literature Comprehension: Grade 3, and Informational Text Comprehension: Grade 2. Jasmine can decode most grade level words and reads with adequate fluency. However, when she is asked literal and inferential questions about a text verbally or through writing, Jasmine struggles to provide accurate answers. She has low reading and writing stamina and requires frequent reminders to utilize comprehension skills such as tracking the text, annotating, rereading, etc. when working independently.

Jasmine benefits immensely from working in small groups or 1 to 1 with teachers. When Jasmine is offered small group instruction in ELA, she chooses to stay in whole group 80% of the time. Jasmine faces significant challenges during whole-group instruction. When faced with grade-level literacy tasks that she finds difficult, she struggles to sustain attention and often disengages by quietly putting her head down or asking to use the restroom multiple times. There is a clear pattern where academic frustration leads to avoidance behaviors. Rather than asking for help, Jasmine tends to shut down, affecting her ability to participate in the general education classroom. While she is successful 1:1, she currently requires support to generalize these skills to group settings.

Jasmine's current ELA grade is a 1.8. Riverstone Prep uses a standards-based grading system where 3 reflects full mastery of grade-level material and 2 reflects partial mastery. Currently, Jasmine is at a third-grade reading level. This makes it difficult for her to understand and write about the rigorous texts in ELA. Jasmine demonstrates partial and inconsistent engagement in class tasks and does not consistently put forth the full effort seen in her best moments during writing assignments. This makes it challenging to collect accurate and reliable data on her independent skills in class. When independently engaged and attempting work with accommodations, she continues to experience difficulty accessing and comprehending grade level texts, e.g., often not annotating, struggling to use reading strategies, etc. She often turns in reading homework with comprehension questions that have answers ranging from 0% to 50% accuracy when completed independently.

Jasmine is new to Riverstone Prep and still learning the writing system. She is still working to create full paragraphs with claims, evidence introduced with context, and analysis that accurately reflects her reasoning for selecting her evidence. While effort is inconsistent, Jasmine is starting to adapt to the writing system and showing growth in finding the value of using graphic organizers and supports such as checklists. Her use of these supports is helping her find success and be more accurate with her responses. Jasmine requires significant adult support to break down complex vocabulary, sentence structures, and key ideas, as well as to organize her thinking for writing tasks. With 1:1 adult support, Jasmine can participate in writing activities at grade level, but her independent performance remains below grade-level expectations with 50% accuracy.
