### Narrative Description of School District Proposal

**1. What action is the school district proposing?**
The school district is proposing the supports, services, and placement outlined in the enclosed IEP and Placement forms.

**2. Why is the school district proposing to act?**
The school district met with the parent and school-based Team on 12/09/25 for a regularly scheduled Annual Review Team Meeting. The Team reviewed Jasmine's progress and continued areas of need and developed the enclosed IEP for continued support and services.

**3. What rejected options were considered?**
The Team considered extended school year services for Jasmine; however, this option was rejected in light of student performance data and records showing that Jasmine is not at risk for substantial regression, or requires a long time to recoup learned skills over the summer holiday.

Jasmine's past evaluation reports were reviewed and used as a basis for the proposed action. Jasmine's grade reports, as well as progress toward IEP goals, were used as a basis for the proposed supports and services as outlined in this IEP.

The Team developed a counseling goal based on Jasmine's needs and included accommodations and services to address her needs, including positive praise and frequent check-ins.

The school district will initiate the supports and services outlined in the enclosed IEP once parental consent of the IEP and placement pages is received.

## Key Profile Summary (MCP Reference)

> This section is not part of the official IEP. It is a structured summary for system use.

**Student:** Jasmine Regina Bailey | Grade 7 | Health Impairment

**Critical gap:** Comprehension is the primary barrier — not decoding. Jasmine can read the words aloud but cannot reliably extract meaning from them. Her informational text comprehension is at a Grade 2 level, meaning grade-level expository essays like those in this unit are significantly inaccessible without scaffolding. The gap between her decoding ability and her comprehension is the defining instructional challenge.

**Present levels by domain:**
- ELA reading: Grade 3 overall (Vocabulary: Gr 3, Literature Comprehension: Gr 3, Informational Text Comprehension: Gr 2)
- Math: Grade 4
- Writing: Below grade level; beginning to use graphic organizers; 50% accuracy independently on comprehension/writing tasks; can perform at grade level with 1:1 adult support

**Accommodations in effect:**
Graphic organizers and checklists | Extra time | Frequent breaks | 1:1 check-ins | Scheduled breaks | Copy of teacher notes | Repeat directions | Reminders to pause/plan/proceed | Front of room seating | Small group (as needed)

**Active IEP goals this lesson touches:**
- Goal 3 (ELA): Comprehension of literal and inferential questions — 50% → 75% accuracy target
- Goal 1 (Counseling): Self-regulation — using calming strategy and returning to task

**Behavioral pattern to know:**
Jasmine does not ask for help when frustrated. Her avoidance signals are physical and behavioral: putting her head down quietly, or requesting bathroom passes multiple times in a row. By the time these appear, frustration is already high. Proactive check-ins before she reaches that threshold are more effective than responding after the signal.

**Instructional entry points (strengths):**
Verbal participation, peer conversation, helping roles and class jobs, 1:1 interactions with adults, specific positive praise (not generic). These are reliable on-ramps for engagement even after a difficult stretch of independent work.
