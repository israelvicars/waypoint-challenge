### Narrative Description of School District Proposal

**1. What action is the school district proposing?**
The school district is proposing the supports, services, and placement outlined in the enclosed IEP and Placement forms.

**2. Why is the school district proposing to act?**
The school district met with the parent and school-based Team on 12/09/25 for a regularly scheduled Annual Review Team Meeting. The Team reviewed Marcus's progress and continued areas of need and developed the enclosed IEP for continued support and services.

**3. What rejected options were considered?**
The Team considered extended school year services for Marcus; however, this option was rejected in light of student performance data showing that Marcus is not at risk for substantial regression over the summer. His reading and written expression skills show consistent maintenance across extended breaks.

**4. What evaluation procedure was used?**
Marcus's past evaluation reports were reviewed and used as a basis for the proposed action. His grades, iReady diagnostic results, curriculum-based writing assessments, and progress toward IEP goals were all considered.

**5. What other factors were relevant?**
Teacher input, parent input, and student input were relevant. The Team discussed Marcus's strong verbal and reasoning skills as significant assets. The Team also considered the impact of slow processing speed and writing rate on his ability to demonstrate his knowledge across all content areas.

**6. What next steps are recommended?**
The school district will initiate supports and services once parental consent is received.

## Measurable Annual Goals

### Goal 1 — Reading Fluency

**Baseline:** Marcus currently reads aloud at approximately 95 words per minute with adequate accuracy (96% word recognition). Grade-level expectation for 7th grade is 140–150 wpm. His slow decoding rate limits his ability to process grade-level text independently within class time constraints, though his comprehension once he has processed the text is near grade level.

| Annual Goal | Criteria | Method | Schedule | Person Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marcus will increase his oral reading fluency rate when reading connected grade-level text aloud. | From 95 wpm to 115 wpm | Oral Reading Fluency probes (1-minute timed reads) | End of each quarter | SE Classroom Teacher |

**Short-term objectives:**
- Marcus will increase his oral reading fluency rate from 95 wpm to 103 wpm by the end of Q2, as measured by curriculum-based oral reading fluency probes.
- Marcus will use pre-reading strategies (scanning headers, reading first and last sentences) to improve comprehension efficiency when reading independently, demonstrating use in 4 out of 5 opportunities.
- Marcus will increase oral reading fluency from 103 wpm to 115 wpm by the end of Q4.

**Progress Reporting:** Parents will be notified every quarter via progress reports alongside general education report cards.

---

### Goal 2 — Written Expression

**Baseline:** When writing independently under timed conditions without accommodations, Marcus consistently produces 2–3 sentences (approximately 30–50 words) in a 15–20 minute in-class writing period. When provided extended time and access to a word processor, output increases to 5–8 sentences (approximately 80–130 words). He has demonstrated understanding of the claim-evidence-analysis writing structure verbally but has not yet applied it consistently in independent written work. His current ELA grade is a 1.6 on Riverstone Prep's standards-based scale (3 = full mastery, 2 = partial mastery), primarily reflecting the gap between his verbal understanding and his written output.

| Annual Goal | Criteria | Method | Schedule | Person Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marcus will increase his independent written output to produce a structured paragraph that includes a clear claim, at least two pieces of textual evidence with context, and a sentence of analysis. | From an average of 2–3 sentences to a full structured paragraph (5–7 sentences) with accommodations | Work samples, curriculum-based writing rubrics | End of each quarter | SE Classroom Teacher |

**Short-term objectives:**
- Marcus will increase his written output from 2–3 sentences to 4–5 sentences using a graphic organizer and word processor in 4 out of 5 writing tasks.
- Marcus will independently write a claim sentence that accurately addresses a prompt in 4 out of 5 opportunities.
- Marcus will select and introduce at least two pieces of textual evidence per writing task, with a context sentence for each, in 4 out of 5 opportunities.
- Marcus will add at least one sentence of analysis connecting his evidence to his claim in 4 out of 5 opportunities.

**Progress Reporting:** Parents will be notified every quarter via progress reports alongside general education report cards.

---

### Goal 3 — Self-Advocacy and Organization

**Baseline:** Marcus is beginning to develop self-advocacy skills. He has requested extended time proactively approximately 40% of the time when it is available. He does not consistently use organizational tools (graphic organizers, outlines) before writing unless prompted by an adult. He sometimes waits until he is visibly frustrated before signaling that he needs support, rather than requesting it earlier in a task.

| Annual Goal | Criteria | Method | Schedule | Person Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marcus will independently initiate the use of a pre-writing graphic organizer or outline before beginning a written response task, and will proactively request available accommodations (extended time, word processor) at the start of an assignment. | In 80% of opportunities | Teacher observation, self-monitoring log | End of each quarter | SE Classroom Teacher / Case Manager |

**Short-term objectives:**
- Given a writing task, Marcus will independently retrieve and begin completing a graphic organizer before writing in 4 out of 5 opportunities.
- Marcus will proactively request extended time or word processor access at the start of a writing task (rather than after frustration) in 4 out of 5 opportunities.
- Marcus will use a self-monitoring checklist (have I planned? have I written my claim? have I added evidence?) to guide his writing process independently in 4 out of 5 opportunities.

**Progress Reporting:** Parents will be notified every quarter via progress reports alongside general education report cards.
